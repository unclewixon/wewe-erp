Why it matters: the same request follows the same path whoever raises it. There is no quiet route around a control.
Built into the chain
Four things you no longer have to remember
01 · SEGREGATION OF DUTIES
You can never approve your own request
Eight rules cover the real conflicts: raising and approving, running a vendor and paying it, preparing and releasing payroll.
02 · DEPARTMENT ROUTING
It goes to your approver, not just any approver
A Programmes request reaches the Programmes head. By default you only see your own department.
03 · DELEGATION
Going away? Hand over formally, for a set period
Thirty days at most. Not to yourself, not passed on again, and both names go on every approval.
04 · ESCALATION
Nothing dies quietly in a queue
Every stage has a clock. Miss it and the item is flagged and pushed upward. You cannot switch that off.
